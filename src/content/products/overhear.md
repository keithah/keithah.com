Overhear lives in your menu bar and answers one question: what's next, and where do I click? It reads the calendars already synced to your Mac, shows the day's meetings, and opens the right join link in the right app.

## Features

- **What's next, at a glance** — the menu bar shows your next meeting and how long until it starts, or a generic "Meeting" label when you'd rather not broadcast the title.
- **One click to join** — join from the menu bar, the meeting list, a reminder, or a keyboard shortcut. Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex and dozens of other services open in their own apps when installed.
- **Details before you commit** — time, organizer, participants and invite notes, with copy-link, open-in-Calendar and hide.
- **Reminders with snooze** — an in-app card or a system notification, cards that fold into a stack you dismiss at once, and optional auto-join at start.
- **Calendar links** — add Google, Outlook, Fastmail or iCloud calendars by their ICS link, without adding the account to macOS Calendar. Refreshes every 15 minutes.
- **Filters that respect your time** — per-calendar selection, tentative and declined events, all-day events, events without links, and regex title filters.
- **Built-in conveniences** — instant meeting creation, bookmarks for recurring rooms, per-calendar colors, duplicate folding when a meeting arrives from two calendars, 12/24-hour time, manual time zone, and open at login.

## Privacy

Overhear reads your calendars on your Mac and never sends your data anywhere. No account, no tracking. The App Store listing declares **Data Not Collected**, and meeting titles are kept out of the macOS log.

## Availability

Free on the Mac App Store for macOS 14.0 or later, 2.1 MB, English, rated 4+. A separate direct-download build adds Sparkle updates. Recording and transcription were removed to keep the app focused on joining meetings.
