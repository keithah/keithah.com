Traverse sells travel eSIMs at [traverse.quest](https://traverse.quest). The idea is simple: your carrier charges $10–15 for every day you use data abroad, while a travel eSIM is one price for the whole trip. Traverse walks you through it — check your phone, pick a plan, pay once, and set it up with pictures.

## Features

- **A price in three questions** — where you're going, for how long, and roughly how much data. The price you see is the price you pay.
- **One price for the trip** — a fixed amount of data (1 GB to 100 GB) or a daily plan, with regional plans for Europe (35 countries) and the whole world (117 countries).
- **Setup you can actually follow** — five steps, each shown on a phone screen, including the settings that stop your carrier from charging you, plus a quick check that your phone will work before you buy.
- **Keep your number** — use Traverse on its own, or alongside your home line so calls and texts still reach you, with advice on what your carrier might charge for.
- **Easy checkout** — pay by card or wallet with no account to create. Your quote is held for 15 minutes, and you can ask for a refund if plans change.
- **Help when you need it** — share or gift an eSIM, top up when you run low, and get help from the built-in assistant, WhatsApp or email.

## Good to know

Traverse is data only: regular calls and texts still go through your home line, at your carrier's rates. Your phone needs to be unlocked and able to use an eSIM. Most plans only start when you first connect abroad, so you can install before you leave.
